Privacy Policy

YouTube Producer Pipeline (YPP-Auto) · Last updated November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Producer Pipeline (YPP-Auto, “we,” “us,” or “the Service”) handles information when you use its video-production workflow tools and optional third-party account connections. For privacy questions or requests, contact vietemt@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • TikTok connection information. If you connect TikTok, the current integration requests the basic profile permission user.info.basic. TikTok may return your account identifier (open_id), display name, and avatar/profile image so the Service can identify and display the connected account.
  • OAuth security and connection data. The sign-in flow processes authorization codes, state and PKCE security values, and provider access credentials needed to create and maintain the connection. OAuth provider tokens are handled server-side; the browser keeps an in-memory session reference rather than storing provider tokens in local storage.
  • Materials you submit. The Service processes the video ideas, scripts, notes, metadata, settings, and other production materials you choose to enter, as well as generated outputs, to provide the workflow features you request.
  • AI feature inputs. When you ask an AI-assisted feature to generate or analyze material, the relevant prompt and input are sent to Google Gemini for processing. TikTok credentials are not used as Gemini prompts.
  • Technical and security data. The Service and its hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, establish and display connections you authorize, provide production-planning and AI-assisted features, protect and troubleshoot the Service, and respond to support or legal requests.

When information is shared

  • TikTok handles your sign-in and authorization and returns the profile information covered by the permission you grant. TikTok’s own terms and privacy policy also apply.
  • Google processes prompts and inputs you submit to Gemini features.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers process information as needed to operate the Service.
  • Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or legal rights.

We do not sell TikTok profile information or use it for advertising. We do not request TikTok video-posting permission in the current basic-profile connection.

Storage, retention, and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the features you request, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Connection credentials are handled on the server and are intended to be removed when you disconnect the account. You can also revoke the Service’s access from your TikTok account settings. To request deletion of information associated with your use of YPP-Auto, email vietemt@gmail.com. Some security records may be retained for a limited period where needed for security or legal purposes.

Your choices

You can choose not to connect TikTok, decline a permission in TikTok’s consent screen, disconnect the account in the Service, or revoke access from TikTok. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information. Contact us at the email above to make a request.

Security

We use server-side handling for OAuth credentials and security measures such as one-time state values and PKCE in the authorization flow. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

YPP-Auto is not designed for children who are not old enough to use the Service and connected third-party platforms under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when the Service or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Questions or privacy requests: vietemt@gmail.com.
